A developer who also runs a business.

Besides writing software, I run a corrugated packaging factory in Karachi. That is where my flagship product, OnePackERP, started: I needed software that understood reels, plies, challans and FBR invoices, so I built it, and now other manufacturers use it too.

That experience shapes every client project. Before writing code, I ask how your day actually runs: who enters the orders, where the paperwork piles up, which numbers you check every evening. Then I build the system around that, not the other way round.
